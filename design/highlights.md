# Game highlights

Games can have notable moments, pivotal moments, and players can perform
particularly noteworthy feats. Various parts of lfdata capture these.

Some highlights happen during the game, some highlights are determined at the
end of a game when considering the final stats.

Some highlights pertain to the game overall, some are for an individual player.

Some highlights are more important than others, and systems that can only show
one or two need to pick based on the order of notability.

### Global game stats highlights

- The game ended in a draw. Both teams have an identical score.
- The two teams ended with a score within 200 points of each other.
- A team was eliminated in less than 8 minutes.
- The other team only has a combined 5 lives or left yet and was thus almost
  eliminated.

### Per-Player stats highlights

- The player is a commander and nuked more than 5 times.
- The player has a hit diff of 1.9 or more.
- The player has 9 or more medic hits.
- The player is the only person on the team to survive.
- The player is the only person on the team to survive, with only 1 or 2 lives
  left.
- The player is a medic and zapped the other medic 3 or more times.
- The player was never zapped.
- The player was zapped less than 5 times the entire game.

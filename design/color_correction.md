# General color correction consideration

Color correction is intended for raw GoPro footage. This footage is primarily
taken in laser tag arenas with GoPro-style cameras. The arenas are typically
very dark but have a lot of bright lights in various places, mostly from the
laser tag packs and from localized lighting throughout the arena, often colored
lighting or UV lighting.

The goal of the color correction is to make the footage easier to see so that it
is not as dark and it is easier to make out the features of the arena and the
players. At the same time, actual bright areas should not be completely
overexposed and washed out, and the footage should still look natural.
